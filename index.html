Before you begin

Complete these requirements before using Skylark:

  • You must have access to Skylark servers. If you do not have access, visit Join.
  • Enable the Shortcuts security and sharing permissions required by your iOS or iPadOS version. Apple may change their names or locations, so follow the instructions for your version.
  • iCloud must be enabled and available because Skylark uses iCloud-based features.
