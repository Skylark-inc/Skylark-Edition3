Before you begin

Complete these requirements before using Skylark:

  • Enable the Shortcuts security and sharing permissions required by your iOS or iPadOS version. Apple may change the names or locations of these settings, so follow instructions for your version.
  • iCloud must be enabled and available because Skylark uses iCloud-based features.
